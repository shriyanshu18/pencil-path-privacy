Pencil Path Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Pencil Path is provided by Shriyanshu gupta. For privacy questions, contact shriyanshu@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Pencil Path stores the name you choose for a local profile, your drawing level, lesson progress, studio preferences, and the journal photos, titles and mood selections you save. This information stays within the app's storage on your device. Local profiles are available to anyone who can open the app on that device.

Photos and camera

You may choose a photo or use your device's camera app to photograph a drawing. Pencil Path uses the selected image to create a journal entry and provide simple feedback about lighting and tonal contrast. Processing happens on your device. Saved journal photos are resized and re-encoded as JPEG. A camera original may remain in the app's private cache until it is replaced or the app's storage is cleared. Your chosen camera or photo provider may also retain its own copy under its own settings.

Data collection and sharing

Pencil Path does not transmit your profiles, drawings or usage to the developer. The app has no advertising, analytics, account registration, cloud synchronization or payment service. It operates offline and requests no Android permissions. Camera and image selection are handled through device apps selected by you.

Security and retention

Data is held in Android app storage. Pencil Path does not add a separate password or application-level encryption to your journal. Keep your device access protected. Journal entries remain until you delete them, delete their profile, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android manifest backup is disabled. Device manufacturers may control some device-transfer behavior.

Deleting your data

To delete a drawing, open Journal, open the drawing, and select Delete drawing. To remove a profile and its journal entries, open your profile menu and choose Delete active profile. To remove all local app data, use Android Settings, Apps, Pencil Path, Storage, Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Pencil Path has no online user accounts.

Children and families

The same local processing and storage practices apply to all users. Pencil Path does not send children's personal information to the developer. A parent or guardian can remove local profiles and drawings using the controls above.

Contacting the developer

If you email shriyanshu@gmail.com, the developer receives the information you include in that email and uses it to respond. Avoid sending sensitive information or private photos unless needed for your request. Email handling is separate from the app's offline operation.

Changes to this policy

This page will show an updated effective date when these practices change.
